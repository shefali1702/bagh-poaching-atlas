Section 09 · The fine print

What is real, and what is not yet.

Exact poaching coordinates are confidential, so every square gets a documented proxy score. The table says which layers are real public data and which are placeholders.

Limitations

  • Labels are proxies, not verified poaching incidents.
  • Reserve shapes are approximate footprints at each reserve's official area.
  • Natural Earth has only major roads and rivers.
  • Elevation, NDVI, forest cover, fragmentation, ranger posts and past pressure are simulated until real rasters and NTCA or MIKE figures are added.
  • It ranks where to look first. Deployment stays with forest officers.
